About {{ SITE_NAME }}

{{ SITE_NAME }} is a modern telemedicine platform developed as a final year project at the Petroleum Training Institute (PTI), Effurun, Delta State. It connects students and staff with licensed healthcare professionals — anytime, anywhere.

Our platform combines AI-powered symptom analysis, electronic medical records, real-time doctor-patient consultations, and IoT health device integration into one secure, easy-to-use system.

  • AI Symptom Checker with 94% accuracy across 11 conditions
  • Real-time chat consultations with licensed doctors
  • Secure electronic medical records and prescriptions
  • BLE health device integration for remote monitoring
  • Role-based access for patients, doctors, and administrators
